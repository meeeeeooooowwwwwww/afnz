Company

Commercial development built around real operating work.

America First Limited is a New Zealand company led by David Ruck from Christchurch. The company works across business and commercial development while also developing its own operating business, GRID EATER, and sponsoring the feasibility-stage CDIP infrastructure project.

What America First does

Help define the commercial problem, then build the path forward.

The work starts with the business rather than a favourite technology or agency category. That can mean clarifying an opportunity, developing a product or offer, restructuring a commercial workstream, introducing practical AI, coordinating implementation, or bringing the right specialist capability around a problem.

America First does not claim to personally supply every technical or professional discipline. Where qualified legal, engineering, security, development or other specialist work is required, the company can help define the requirement, coordinate the contribution and keep it connected to the commercial objective.

How the company operates

AI is part of the operating model, not a marketing costume.

America First uses authorised AI systems as part of its own research, knowledge, planning and execution workflows. That internal operating work informs how the company approaches practical AI integration for other businesses: define the purpose, keep a human owner, control information and permissions, test the workflow, document it and review whether it actually improves the work.

Where a client environment requires deeper software, security, legal, privacy or specialist technical input, that dependency is made explicit rather than hidden behind an AI label.
