First contact

Keep the first message simple.

Useful information includes your organisation, the problem or opportunity, the broad area of work and any timing that genuinely matters. Budget is optional at the first-contact stage.

Please do not send sensitive material in the first enquiry.

Do not send passwords, API keys, payment credentials, confidential datasets, sensitive customer or employee records, or other protected source material. If confidential information is genuinely required later, the handling method can be agreed first.

America First Limited does not publish a fixed enquiry-response SLA. The current operation is founder-led and enquiries are handled according to fit, urgency and current commitments.
