THE FOUNDER

David Ruck

Founder of America First Limited. A career across sales, telecommunications, media, business development and digital services now informs his work directing AI-assisted businesses and projects.

THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND AMERICA FIRST

A career built across customers, operations and technology.

SERVICE, AUDIENCES & PRODUCTION

Learning how the whole experience works.

David began in hospitality, where preparation, timing and consistency directly shape the customer experience. His work later extended into Surreal events, ALT TV production and radio sales and programming at PulzarFM.

These roles brought together audience-building, commercial relationships and practical delivery. They developed an understanding of how an idea reaches people, earns their attention and becomes an experience worth returning to.

SALES DEVELOPMENT & LEADERSHIP

Building the process behind the performance.

Hotel Dynamics Group provided a foundation in telephone sales, qualification and objection handling. At TelstraClear, David developed outbound telephone sales through scripts, live-call refinement and team training.

At HRV’s Auckland East and Central franchise operations, he rebuilt appointment-setting through call structure, coaching and closer alignment with field sales. These experiences shaped his ability to diagnose a commercial problem and turn the solution into a process a team can use.

VENTURES & BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Taking responsibility for the whole operation.

His own ventures extended that experience into business ownership and development. Red Vs Blue Gaming combined a new identity and customer environment with technical restoration. Mojo Marketing carried his sales and marketing work into increasingly digital services.

In Wellington, 0199 brought business data, telecommunications, inbound service and outbound sales together. Each venture required connecting the proposition with the people, systems and everyday decisions that made it work.

DIGITAL SERVICES & AI

Bringing accumulated experience into the present.

Demand from directory customers led into websites, email and hosting through Real Websites, followed by broader work in search, digital marketing and automation. Today, those threads converge in America First, GRID EATER and the feasibility-stage CDIP project.

David now directs AI-assisted research and development with the same practical focus: understand the objective, identify weak assumptions, connect the right capabilities and review the result. Technology expands what can be explored and built; experience gives that work direction.
