WHEN TO BRING US IN

Useful when the work crosses normal supplier boundaries.

  • The business knows AI matters but does not yet have a useful operating plan.
  • Knowledge is scattered across people, files, systems or inboxes.
  • Manual workflows can be assisted or automated without losing human accountability.
  • Management needs a controlled path from experiments into reliable operating use.
WHAT WE CAN DO

Practical scope around the real problem.

  • Map workflows, information, decisions and human ownership.
  • Identify high-value AI use cases and sensible implementation sequence.
  • Prepare knowledge/data structures and permission boundaries.
  • Select suitable models/tools for the use case.
  • Design human-review, escalation and operating controls.
  • Prototype or coordinate bounded implementation with client IT/vendors where appropriate.
TYPICAL OUTPUTS

Clear enough that people know what is actually being delivered.

  • AI opportunity/workflow map.
  • Knowledge and information requirements.
  • Tool/model selection rationale.
  • Human-control and permission design.
  • Pilot or implementation plan.
  • Operating documentation and review criteria.
