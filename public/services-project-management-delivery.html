WHEN TO BRING US IN

Useful when the work crosses normal supplier boundaries.

  • A project is important but lacks a clear delivery structure.
  • Multiple teams, suppliers or specialists need coordination.
  • Governance and reporting are consuming time without improving decisions.
  • A project has drifted and needs an independent review or recovery plan.
WHAT WE CAN DO

Practical scope around the real problem.

  • Define scope, objectives, success and acceptance criteria.
  • Build workstream, responsibility and dependency structure.
  • Establish practical governance, reporting and decision control.
  • Coordinate suppliers, vendors and specialist contributors.
  • Track issues, risks, actions and implementation follow-through.
  • Support handover, closeout or project-recovery planning.
TYPICAL OUTPUTS

Clear enough that people know what is actually being delivered.

  • Project brief and delivery roadmap.
  • Workstream and responsibility map.
  • Issue, risk, action and dependency controls.
  • Governance/reporting pack.
  • Supplier and specialist briefs.
  • Closeout, handover or recovery plan.
