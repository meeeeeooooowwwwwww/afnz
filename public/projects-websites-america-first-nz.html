COMPANY MEDIA PRESENCE · VIDEO-LED WEBSITE

America First NZ: a focused video-led company presence

A lightweight site that gives featured company media the main visual priority with concise supporting links.

WHY THE PROJECT EXISTS

Designed around its particular audience.

Visitors arriving for a specific company video or seeking related company information.

What the work covers.

A media-first homepage, minimal navigation, responsive embed presentation and contextual onward paths to the main corporate website and related ventures.

How the website is delivered.

The design deliberately avoids rebuilding the full corporate site under another domain. Media can lead while supporting links help visitors find the appropriate company and project pages.

ROLE AND EVIDENCE

Clear responsibilities, no invented results.

A media showcase is distinct from customer project delivery. Third-party video content and availability remain controlled by its original provider.
