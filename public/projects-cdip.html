CDIP · FEASIBILITY STAGE

Developing the case for Canterbury’s digital infrastructure.

CDIP is America First’s feasibility-stage data-centre project. We are assessing how customer demand, power, connectivity and commercial requirements could support staged development in Canterbury.

CONNECTED WORKSTREAMS

Evaluate the project as a whole.

Demand, power, connectivity, site conditions and the commercial model shape each other. We assess them together so development decisions reflect the requirements of a viable operation.

CURRENT WORK

Establish the evidence for the next stage.

The project is at feasibility stage. Current work centres on requirements discovery, option assessment and specialist input, with site, infrastructure and funding decisions still to be resolved.

DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

Build the path around demand.

We are exploring a staged approach that connects practical entry options with future expansion. America First leads commercial development and coordination, bringing qualified technical specialists into the assessment as the project requires.
