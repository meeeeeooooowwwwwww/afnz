A project sponsored by America First Limited

CDIP

Developing the case for New Zealand data-centre infrastructure.

The project

Feasibility before commitment.

CDIP is a data-centre development project at feasibility stage. The work is to establish how customer demand, power, connectivity, a suitable site and technical design can support a commercially viable development.

Current focus

Infrastructure requirements and commercial development.

The project is evaluating infrastructure requirements and engaging relevant specialists and counterparties. Site suitability, delivery requirements and the commercial model are being considered together.

A long-term proposition

Capability that has to earn its way forward.

The ambition is to contribute to New Zealand's digital infrastructure and capability. Development decisions will depend on the results of the feasibility work.
