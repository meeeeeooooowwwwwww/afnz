A project sponsored by America First Limited · Feasibility stage

Feasibility before commitment.

CDIP is developing the case for additional New Zealand data-centre capability in Canterbury. The current job is not to sell an imagined finished facility; it is to establish whether the technical, commercial and customer conditions support a viable staged development.

What the project is testing

The project has to work as a system.

A data-centre development is only credible if the major dependencies line up. CDIP is assessing customer demand alongside power availability, fibre and route diversity, suitable site conditions, technical design, operating requirements and the commercial model.

The work is structured to expose weak assumptions early rather than commit capital first and discover the constraints later.

Development approach

Staged, demand-led and evidence-led.

The project direction is to preserve a path for meaningful growth while keeping initial development proportionate to evidenced demand and financeability.

Technical, utility, connectivity, site, customer and commercial questions are treated as connected workstreams. Specialist engineering and technical outputs remain with appropriately qualified providers.

Current status

Feasibility stage means feasibility stage.

CDIP is not presented as an operational data centre. The public project description does not imply that a final site, power allocation, fibre architecture, construction programme, customer commitment, funding package or compute capacity has already been secured.

Development decisions will be made against the evidence produced by the feasibility work.

Why America First is developing it

Digital demand and infrastructure are connected over the long term.

America First Limited is already building digital business, data and service capability through GRID EATER. CDIP tests whether deeper New Zealand infrastructure can become a commercially credible extension of that direction over time.

The project must earn its way forward on technical and commercial evidence rather than strategic ambition alone.
