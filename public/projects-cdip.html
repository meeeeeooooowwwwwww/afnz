CDIP · FEASIBILITY STAGE

Feasibility before commitment.

CDIP is testing whether customer demand, power, connectivity, site conditions, technical design and commercial structure can support a viable staged data-centre development in Canterbury.

CONNECTED WORKSTREAMS

The project has to work as a system.

Demand, power, connectivity, site, technical design and the commercial model are assessed together because weakness in one can change the viability of the rest.

CURRENT STATUS

Feasibility stage means feasibility stage.

CDIP is not presented as an operational data centre. The public project description does not imply that a final site, power allocation, fibre architecture, construction programme, customer commitment, funding package or compute capacity has already been secured.

DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

Staged, demand-led and evidence-led.

Development decisions are intended to follow evidence rather than ambition. Specialist engineering and technical outputs remain with appropriately qualified providers.
