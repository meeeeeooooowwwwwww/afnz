Kiwi Gardeners: a useful local website for gardening enquiries

A Christchurch-focused website developed with suburb information, garden guides, clear service navigation and a protected enquiry form.

WHY THE PROJECT EXISTS

Designed around its particular audience.

Christchurch residents looking for local garden maintenance information and a way to contact a gardener.

What the work covers.

Website development, responsive layouts, suburb and garden-guide navigation, on-page search foundations, and a contact path that is protected against automated abuse.

How the website is delivered.

The website is served through Cloudflare Workers with locally cached static assets. Its contact endpoint operates separately and is not cached with public HTML.

ROLE AND EVIDENCE

Clear responsibilities, no invented results.

Dallas Timpson is the independent gardener who provides gardening work and quotes. America First Limited's involvement concerns website and digital development. The project does not claim search rankings or completed gardening jobs.
