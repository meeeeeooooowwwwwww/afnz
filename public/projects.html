PREVIOUS PROJECTS

Earlier ventures. Experience that carries forward.

These ventures were led by our founder before America First’s current work. Together they connect customer acquisition, brand development, technology and hands-on operations.

BUSINESS REVIVAL & BRAND DEVELOPMENT

Red Vs Blue Gaming

Reviving the former CUBE gaming centre in West Auckland brought the technical operation, physical environment and customer community together. Our founder led the rebrand and renewal of the space, working with a network engineer to restore the computers and network.

The project demonstrates a practical instinct that still informs America First: recognise what an audience values, coordinate the right expertise and make the whole experience work.

SALES & MARKETING DEVELOPMENT

Mojo Marketing

Mojo Marketing was a vehicle for sales, marketing and an increasing range of digital work. It marked the move from individual sales roles towards acquisition systems, campaigns and repeatable offers.

That focus on how customers are reached—and how an offer becomes a working business—continues in America First’s commercial development approach.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS & BUSINESS SYSTEMS

0199 Directory Assistance

The Wellington 0199 operation connected a free-to-caller telephone number, business data, a VoIP call centre, inbound directory assistance and outbound sales. Our founder developed the commercial offer and sales operation, coordinating telecommunications capability with specialist providers.

Customer demand then extended the work into websites, email and hosting. Those connections between discovery, customer acquisition and digital delivery now inform GRID EATER.

FOUNDER’S SALES DEVELOPMENT EXPERIENCE

Turning sales judgement into repeatable performance.

Earlier roles at TelstraClear and HRV developed the approach to customer acquisition, process design and team development that informs America First today.

OUTBOUND SALES DEVELOPMENT

TelstraClear

Our founder developed an outbound telephone-sales operation at TelstraClear, adapting a direct-sales framework for telecommunications. The work included writing scripts, developing qualification and objection handling through live calls, and training the team around the sales process.

It brought offer design, customer trust and repeatable execution into one commercial system.

Read the TelstraClear story ↗
APPOINTMENT-SETTING & TEAM DEVELOPMENT

HRV

At the Auckland East and Auckland Central HRV franchise operations, our founder rebuilt telephone appointment-setting around a clear objective: qualified appointments that supported the field-sales team. The work combined call structure, scripting, coaching and coordination between the two teams.

That experience reinforces a principle we apply today: define the right outcome, then align the people, process and feedback around it.
