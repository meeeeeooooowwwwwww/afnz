WHEN TO BRING US IN

Useful when the work crosses normal supplier boundaries.

  • An opportunity is attractive but still rests on material assumptions.
  • A project needs requirements before technical or commercial commitments are made.
  • Several options need to be compared coherently.
  • Management needs a decision gate rather than another open-ended research exercise.
WHAT WE CAN DO

Practical scope around the real problem.

  • Frame the problem, decision and evidence standard.
  • Gather and organise relevant research and source evidence.
  • Run stakeholder, supplier and requirements discovery.
  • Map options, assumptions, risks and dependencies.
  • Structure commercial/operational feasibility and business-case work.
  • Identify specialist analysis still required before a go/no-go decision.
TYPICAL OUTPUTS

Clear enough that people know what is actually being delivered.

  • Discovery and requirements brief.
  • Evidence and assumptions register.
  • Options and dependency map.
  • Feasibility findings.
  • Business-case structure.
  • Decision gates and next-action plan.
BOUNDARIES

No invented expertise. No guaranteed outcomes.

This work is not a formal valuation, regulated investment opinion, engineering assurance, legal/tax/accounting opinion or security certification. Where those are needed, the requirement is identified and routed to the appropriate qualified provider.

Commercial structure, scope, specialist dependencies, timing and any ongoing advisory arrangement are agreed for the engagement. No standard public price or fixed service-level promise applies.
